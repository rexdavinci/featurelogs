# Sharing FeatureLogs 0.2.2

Suggested order, researched on 2026-10-19. These are places to share the project, not hosting recommendations. No submissions have been made.

1. **GitHub release first.** Publish the 0.2.2 changelog with screenshots, a short example, and a working self-hosting path. Add accurate topics such as `product-updates`, `changelog`, `open-source`, `nextjs`, and `ai`. GitHub's [topic documentation](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/managing-your-repositorys-settings-and-features/customizing-your-repository/classifying-your-repository-with-topics) explains how topics help people find relevant repositories.
2. **Show HN for contributor feedback.** Lead with the working product and the open-source/provider-independent approach. Suggested title: “Show HN: FeatureLogs — open-source product updates with swappable AI providers.” Link a usable example and the repository. The [Show HN guidelines](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html) favor things people can try, ideally without signup; the public interactive examples help, but a no-signup generation sandbox would offer a more complete trial. Ask for feedback on the workflow and architecture.
3. **Product Hunt for the broader product audience.** Show how one set of notes becomes updates for different channels. Prepare readable screenshots, a short walkthrough, and a maker comment explaining the problem and why the tool exists. Follow the official [launch guide](https://www.producthunt.com/launch). Describe workflow agents as a future direction; they are not part of 0.2.2.

For ongoing recognition, a practical write-up about the adapter design and what changed in 0.2.2 can give contributors a concrete reason to explore the repository. Keep the initial launch focused on what works today.
